IMAGE OPTIMIZATION TIPS:
-----------------------

1. Compress images before using them:
   - TinyPNG: https://tinypng.com/
   - Squoosh: https://squoosh.app/
   - ImageOptim (Mac): https://imageoptim.com/

2. Use appropriate formats:
   - PNG: For logos and images with transparency
   - JPG: For photos and complex images
   - WebP: For modern browsers (optional)

3. Recommended file sizes:
   - Logo: Under 50KB
   - Hero background: Under 500KB
   - Client images: Under 200KB each
